Third-party models bundled with Anaglyphohol
============================================

Anaglyphohol ships two depth estimation models inside the extension (app/models/...). Both are licensed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0; the full license text is in APACHE-2.0.txt next to this file. Neither model is
affiliated with or endorses Anaglyphohol.


Depth Anything 3 - Small (still images)
---------------------------------------
Files:    models/depth-anything-v3-small/onnx/model.onnx, model.onnx_data
Source:   https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/depth-anything-v3-small (ONNX conversion of Depth Anything 3 Small)
Project:  https://github.com/ByteDance-Seed/Depth-Anything-3
License:  Apache License 2.0
Changes:  none - the files are shipped as published.


Video Depth Anything - Small (video)
------------------------------------
Files:    models/video-depth-anything-small/model.onnx
Source:   https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Video-Depth-Anything-Small (video_depth_anything_vits.pth)
Project:  https://github.com/DepthAnything/Video-Depth-Anything
Paper:    S. Chen, H. Guo, S. Zhu, F. Zhang, Z. Huang, J. Feng, B. Kang,
          "Video Depth Anything: Consistent Depth Estimation for Super-Long Videos", 2025.
License:  Apache License 2.0 (the Small model only; Video Depth Anything Base/Large are CC-BY-NC-4.0 and are NOT
          included). Its DINOv2 encoder code is Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates.
Changes:  MODIFIED. The PyTorch weights were converted to ONNX by SpawnDev.ILGPU.ML's tools/vda-export
          (https://github.com/LostBeard/SpawnDev.ILGPU.ML), which exports ONE streaming step of the model:
          - the per-frame temporal cache holds each attention layer's key/value projections instead of its hidden
            states (mathematically equivalent; less work per frame),
          - the attention core is written as softmax(q k^T * scale) v instead of baddbmm,
          - the positional-embedding interpolation, tensor rearranges and reshapes are expressed so the graph accepts
            any input size (multiples of 14) instead of the export size.
          The weights' values are unchanged.
